# Roles at a glance

<p class="lead">A role is a set of permissions. ImpactMEL comes with seven built-in roles. Each opens on its own home page with its own menu, so people see the work that is theirs first.</p>

## The built-in roles

| Role | Home page shows | Can |
|---|---|---|
| [Organisation admin](/roles/organisation-admin) | Your team, invitations, workspace and setup shortcuts | Everything, including people, roles, workspace settings, integrations and billing |
| [Director](/roles/director) | The portfolio, indicators at risk, risks, reports to sign off | Read everything, approve figures, judge results, approve budgets, publish reports, decide on risks |
| [M&E officer](/roles/me-officer) | Review queue, reporting periods, programme performance, deadlines | Build frameworks, indicators and forms, review and approve figures, close periods, write and publish reports |
| [Programme manager](/roles/programme-manager) | This week's activities, deadlines and project progress | Manage their projects, activities, budgets and risks |
| [Field officer](/roles/field-officer) | Figures to correct or send, and forms to fill | Send figures and form responses, correct returned figures |
| [Finance officer](/roles/finance-officer) | Spending this period, explanations due, sign-offs waiting | Set up budgets, record spending, write variance explanations |
| [Viewer](/roles/viewer-and-funder) | A read-only overview of the portfolio | Read results, dashboards and published reports |

Funders have their own workspace with separate roles. See [Viewer and funder](/roles/viewer-and-funder) and [Funder workspace](/manual/funders).

## Rules that always apply

- **The owner.** The person who creates the workspace is its owner and keeps full access. Nobody can remove or demote the last owner. Ownership can be transferred from the People page.
- **Several roles.** A person can hold several roles. Their access is everything those roles allow together. Their home page follows the role chosen as **Opens on**, and they can change it with **Work as** in the user menu.
- **No self-promotion.** Only owners can make someone an owner or Organisation admin. Anyone else who manages people can only give access they already have, never to themselves.
- **Assigned projects.** Field officers and other roles limited to their projects see only the projects they are assigned to. Someone with no project assignment sees an empty portfolio.
- **Individual plan.** On the Individual plan, the one person holds every role and approves their own work.

## Changing what a role can do

Administrators can change the permissions of any role, including the built-in ones, and create new roles. See [Roles and permissions](/manual/administration#roles-and-permissions).
