# Finance officer

<p class="lead">The Finance officer keeps budgets and spending in ImpactMEL alongside results, so reports carry money and results together. ImpactMEL is a control layer over your accounting system, not a replacement for it.</p>

![The Finance officer's home page.](/images/role-finance-home.webp)

## Home page

- **Spending this period.** Projects with spending entered for the current period.
- **Explanations due.** Budget lines beyond the variance threshold that still need an explanation.
- **Lines off pace.** Lines spending more or less than plan to date.
- **Waiting on a director.** Budget versions to approve and spending periods to sign off.

## Menu

Home, Programmes, Reports. The finance officer can read programmes, results and reports, but not forms, figures or people.
