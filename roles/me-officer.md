# M&E officer

<p class="lead">The M&E officer builds the measurement system and keeps the data trustworthy: the results framework, indicators and targets, forms, the review queue, reporting periods and reports.</p>

![The M&E officer's home page.](/images/role-me-home.webp)

## Home page

- **Indicators on track**, **Waiting for review**, **figures approved** for the current period, and **Next deadline**.
- **Reporting periods.** For each open period, how many indicators have a figure in and how many are approved, with what is outstanding and a **Close period** button. **Manage** opens all periods.
- **Waiting for your review**, oldest first, with **Start reviewing**.
- **How each programme is doing**, **Due soon**, indicators behind target, and risks.

## Menu

Home, Programmes, Review, Results framework, Indicators, Analysis, Forms, Participants, Reports. Integrations open from Forms, and the activity log opens from Review's **History** tab.
