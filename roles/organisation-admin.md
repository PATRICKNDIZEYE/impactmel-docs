# Organisation admin

<p class="lead">The Organisation admin looks after the workspace: who is in it, what each person can do, how the workspace is set up, and how it is paid for.</p>

![The Organisation admin home page.](/images/role-admin-home.webp)

## Home page

- **Shortcut cards** for People, Roles and permissions, Sign-in and security, Workspace, Integrations and Activity log, each with a short status.
- **Your team**, listing members and their roles, with **Invite people**.
- **Workspace**, a summary of your plan, country, currency and reporting calendar.
- **Getting started**, a checklist for a new workspace.

## Menu

Home, Programmes, Analysis, People, Roles and permissions, Workspace, Integrations, Plans and billing, Sign-in and security, Activity log.
