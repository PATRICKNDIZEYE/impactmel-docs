# Director

<p class="lead">The Director sees the whole portfolio on one page: how close each programme is to its targets, which indicators and risks need attention, and which reports are waiting for sign-off.</p>

![The Director's Overview.](/images/role-director-home.webp)

## Overview page

- **Target achieved.** The average progress across all indicators with approved figures.
- **Indicators on track.** How many are on track, at risk and off track, against targets that have fallen due.
- **Active risks.** The count, with critical and high risks and overdue reviews.
- **Budget spent.** Spending to date against approved budgets and time gone.
- **Programmes**, worst first by indicators on track. Drafts do not count.
- **Needs your sign-off.** Reports ready to publish, budgets to approve and risk decisions.
- **Critical risks** and **Coming up** (the next deadlines).

Every number opens the list behind it.

## Menu

Overview, Programmes, Locations, Indicators, Analysis, Review, Reports. Directors also see the workspace pages their permissions open.

## What a Director does

- [Approve or return figures](/manual/review), including as second approver where a project needs two.
- [Record a performance judgement](/manual/results-framework#performance-judgements) for a result.
- [Approve a budget version](/manual/budgets#versions-and-approval) and sign off spending periods.
- [Decide on an escalated risk](/manual/risks#escalate-and-decide).
- [Publish a report](/manual/reports#publish-a-report) and create a read-only link for a funder.
