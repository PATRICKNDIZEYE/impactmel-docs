# Field officer

<p class="lead">The Field officer collects and sends figures. Their home page is a task list: figures returned for correction first, then drafts to send, then forms to fill.</p>

![The Field officer's home page, My tasks.](/images/role-field-home.webp)

## My tasks

- **Returned** figures come first, with the reviewer's reason and a **Correct** button.
- **Draft** and **Overdue** figures you have started, with **Edit** and **Send**.
- **To fill** forms your organisation has published, with **Fill**.
- **New figure** starts a figure for one of your indicators and an open period.
- The footer says which reporting period is open.
- **Last sent** shows what happened to your recent figures.

## Menu

My tasks, Forms, Sent, Programmes. Field officers see only the projects they are assigned to.
