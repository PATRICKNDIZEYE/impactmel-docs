# Programme manager

<p class="lead">The Programme manager runs projects day to day: activities, deadlines, budgets and risks, with progress against targets always in view.</p>

![The Programme manager's home page.](/images/role-pm-home.webp)

## Home page

- **This week.** Activities due this week and anything overdue. Tick an activity to mark it done, or open **All activities**.
- **Upcoming deadlines.** Reporting periods, activity end dates, risk actions and reviews, soonest first.
- **Project cards** for up to three of your projects, showing indicators on track, average progress and activities done. Drafts are excluded.

## Menu

Home, Programmes, Activities, Deadlines, Indicators, Analysis, Reports, and **My projects**, which lists the projects you manage, or the ones you are on if you manage none.
