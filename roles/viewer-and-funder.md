# Viewer and funder

<p class="lead">Viewers read results without changing anything. Funders who work with several organisations have a separate funder workspace.</p>

## Viewer

The Viewer role is for board members, external reviewers and donor contacts inside your workspace. Viewers see an Overview like the Director's, without approvals, for the programmes and projects they are assigned to.

![The Viewer's Overview.](/images/role-viewer-home.webp)

Menu: Overview, Programmes, Locations, Indicators, Analysis, Reports. Viewers can read published reports and dashboards, and cannot change anything.

## Sharing without an account

You do not need to make someone a Viewer to show them results. You can send:

- a [read-only report link](/manual/reports#share-a-report), which opens one published report with its downloads; or
- a [public dashboard link](/manual/sharing-dashboards), which shows a dashboard's approved figures and expires when you choose.

## Funder workspace

A funder organisation can have its own ImpactMEL workspace. It has its own roles (Funder admin, M&E lead, Programme officer and Board viewer) and its own menu: **Portfolio**, **Grantees**, **Requests** and **Reports**. Funders see only what each grantee chooses to share with them. See [Funder workspace](/manual/funders).
