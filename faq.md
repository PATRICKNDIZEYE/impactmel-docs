# Frequently asked questions

## Getting started

### Is there a free plan?
No. Every cloud plan can start with a 30-day free trial, without a card. See [Plans](/plans).

### How long does it take to set up?
Creating a workspace takes a few minutes. Setting up a programme, its framework, indicators and targets depends on how much you have ready. On the Custom plan, we set it up with you.

### Can we bring our existing logframe?
Yes. Build it in the [results framework](/manual/results-framework), or, if AI features are on, upload your proposal or logframe document and choose **Draft from a proposal**.

### Can we import figures we already have?
Yes. Import figures from Excel or CSV, and participants from CSV or Excel. See [Integrations](/manual/integrations#excel-and-csv).

## Using ImpactMEL

### Why doesn't my figure show on the dashboard?
Only approved figures count. Check whether it is still a draft, waiting in [Review](/manual/review), or returned to you.

### Why is an indicator shown as "No target"?
It has no target for the period, or no target has fallen due yet. Indicators without targets are not counted as off track. See [Indicators](/manual/indicators#which-figures-count).

### How is progress worked out?
With a baseline: (actual − baseline) ÷ (target − baseline). Without one: actual ÷ target. See [the progress formula](/manual/indicators#the-progress-formula).

### Can we change the on-track and at-risk thresholds?
Yes. The defaults are on track at 90% or more and at risk from 70%. Ask ImpactMEL support to set your organisation's thresholds; there is no screen for this yet.

### Can a project need two approvals?
Yes. Ask ImpactMEL support to set it for the project. The review queue then shows "1 of 2", and a figure is final after the second approval.

### Can I see only my projects?
Field officers and other roles limited to their projects see only the projects they are assigned to. Programme managers see their own projects first.

### I hold two roles. How do I switch?
Choose your name at the bottom of the sidebar, then **Work as**. See [Finding your way](/getting-started/finding-your-way).

### Can I still use the old interface?
Yes. Choose **Switch to classic interface** in the user menu. You can switch back at any time.

## Field collection

### Do forms work offline?
Partly. Opening a form needs a connection. Once it is open, answers are kept on the phone and sending waits until the connection returns. See [what happens without a connection](/manual/collecting-data#what-happens-without-a-connection).

### Do partners need an account to send data?
No. Share a form's public link. Anyone with the link can fill it in, and responses still go through review.

### Can we use KoboToolbox or DHIS2?
Yes. See [Integrations](/manual/integrations).

## Reports and sharing

### Can we edit figures in a report?
No. Reports are built from approved figures. To change a figure, correct it through review and refresh the report.

### What does the fingerprint on a published report mean?
It is a SHA-256 hash of the report's figures, layout and text at publication. If anything changed, the fingerprint would change. See [Publish a report](/manual/reports#publish-a-report).

### Can a funder see our reports without an account?
Yes. Publish the report and create a read-only link. Dashboards can also be shared by link. See [Reports](/manual/reports#share-a-report) and [Sharing dashboards](/manual/sharing-dashboards).

## Data and security

### Who owns our data?
Your organisation does, and it can be exported at any time. See [Security and data](/security).

### Can we run ImpactMEL on our own servers?
Yes. See [Plans](/plans) and [hosting and residency](https://www.impactmel.com/trust/hosting-and-residency).

### Can we turn the AI off?
Yes. An Organisation admin can switch it off for everyone in Workspace settings. See [AI features](/manual/ai#switch-ai-off).
