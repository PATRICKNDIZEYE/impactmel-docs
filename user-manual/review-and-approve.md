# Review and approve

Every figure your team reports waits for a decision before it counts. **Review** in the top bar is where those decisions are made.

Approving a figure publishes it. It then appears in your dashboards, in the indicator register and in any report you assemble.

Open it from the top bar: **Review**.

{{figure:review-queue}}

---

## What the queue holds

The page groups figures by project and period, so you decide a whole quarter of one project at a time. Each row shows:

- The indicator name. Click it to open the indicator.
- The figure reported, with its unit. If the submission holds more than one entry, the row says how many.
- Who entered it, and how long ago.
- The due date, if the period has one.
- A **Sent back** badge if it has already been returned once, with the reason underneath.

---

## The four views

Four buttons across the top switch what the list contains. Each carries a count, so you can see where the work is without clicking.

| View | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Waiting for me** | Figures you have not given a decision on yet |
| **All in review** | Everything submitted or sent back |
| **Asked of me** | Figures a colleague has asked you to look at |
| **Sent back** | Figures waiting for the person who entered them to correct |

The page opens on **Waiting for me**.

The dropdown on the right narrows the list to one project.

## Approving a figure

1. Find the row.
2. Click **Approve**.

If the project needs only one approver, that is the end of it. A message confirms the figure is now part of your reported data.

### When a project needs two approvers

Some projects require more than one approval before a figure is published. On those rows you see a badge reading, for example, **1 of 2 approved**.

- Approve as normal. A message tells you how many more approvals are still needed.
- Your own row then reads *Your approval is in — waiting for 1 more*, and the Approve button is gone. You cannot approve the same figure twice.
- Another approver has to open the same row and approve it before the figure is published.

Click the chevron at the end of a row to open **Decisions so far**. It lists each decision, who made it, when, and the reason if one was given.

{{figure:review-approval-chain}}

::: warning No screen sets the number of approvers
How many approvals a project needs is set on the project record, not from a page in the application. Ask your ImpactMEL contact to change it.
:::

## Sending a figure back

Send a figure back when it needs correcting rather than rejecting outright.

1. Click **Send back** on the row.
2. Type what needs to change in **What needs to change?** This is required — you cannot send a figure back without a reason.
3. Click **Send back**.

{{figure:review-send-back}}

The person who entered the figure is told by email and in the application. Your reason is kept with the submission, so it stays on the record and shows on the row in the queue.

They can correct the figure and submit it again.

### Reject instead of return

In the same dialog there is a checkbox: **Reject rather than return**. Use it when the figure should not have been reported at all, rather than being wrong.

The person can still revise it either way. The difference is in the log, which keeps rejections and returns apart.

---

## Ask someone to look at it

Some figures need a person who knows the field better than you do.

1. Click **Ask someone** on the row.
2. Choose a colleague from **Who should look at it?**
3. Click **Ask them**.

{{figure:review-ask-someone}}

They are told straight away, by email and in the application. The row gains a badge reading **With** and their name, so nobody else picks it up by mistake. It appears in their **Asked of me** view.

When they are finished, you see it back in your own queue.

## What a reporter sees here

You can open Review and see the queue, which is the fastest way to find out what has been **sent back** to you and why.

You cannot approve a figure, send one back, or ask a colleague to review one. Those are M&E officer and org admin actions.

To correct something that was sent back, read the reason on the row, then go to the project's **Progress Reports** and submit the figure again.

<!-- /roles -->

---

## Seeing the whole submission

The expanded row has a link, **Open the full submission**. It takes you to the Submissions screen with that submission open, where you can read the narrative, the disaggregated breakdown and any evidence attached to it.

---

## What happens to notifications

These emails send themselves, without anyone choosing to send them:

- The person who entered a figure is emailed when it is approved.
- They are emailed when it is sent back, and the email carries your reason.
- They are emailed when a comment is added to their submission.
- A colleague is emailed when you ask them to review something.

---

## When the queue is empty

If nothing is waiting on you the page says so. Figures appear here as soon as somebody submits them.

An empty queue on **All in review** means nothing anywhere has been submitted — which, near a deadline, is worth checking against **Period sign-off**.
