# Introduction

<p class="lead">ImpactMEL is monitoring, evaluation and learning software for organisations that have to show what their programmes achieved, and prove it.</p>

It holds your results framework, indicators and targets, collects figures from the field, puts every figure through review, and assembles donor reports from the approved record. Directors, M&E teams, programme staff, field teams, finance staff and funders all work in the same workspace, each with the screens their job needs.

## Who it is for

- **National and international NGOs** reporting to several donors from one set of figures.
- **Government programmes and agencies** that need a clear approval trail. ImpactMEL can also run on your own servers.
- **Consortiums** where partners report into one framework.
- **Funders** who want consistent results from the organisations they fund.
- **Individual M&E consultants and officers** who report on their own work.

## Key ideas

### One approved record

Every figure in ImpactMEL has a status. It starts as a draft, is sent for review, and is either returned with a reason or approved. Only approved figures count in dashboards, progress and reports. The same approved figure feeds every report and every donor format, so the number you send one donor is the number you send the next.

Once approved, a figure is locked. Every change before that point is kept in its history, with who made it and when.

### Plan, collect, check, report

The product follows the order of the work:

| Stage | What you do | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Plan | Set up programmes and projects, build the results framework, define indicators with baselines and targets | [Programmes and projects](/manual/programmes-and-projects), [Results framework](/manual/results-framework), [Indicators](/manual/indicators) |
| Collect | Build forms, collect from phones and links, register participants, track activities | [Forms](/manual/forms), [Collecting data](/manual/collecting-data), [Participants](/manual/participants) |
| Check | Review figures with automatic checks, approve or return them, close and lock periods | [Review and data quality](/manual/review), [Reporting periods](/manual/reporting-periods) |
| Report | Analyse, build dashboards, assemble and publish reports, share them by link | [Analysis](/manual/analysis), [Reports](/manual/reports) |

### Roles

People are given roles, and a role is a set of permissions. ImpactMEL comes with seven built-in roles: Organisation admin, Director, M&E officer, Programme manager, Field officer, Finance officer and Viewer. Each role opens on its own home page with its own menu. A person can hold several roles and gets everything those roles allow together. Administrators can change any role or create new ones. See [Roles at a glance](/roles/).

### Reporting periods

Figures and targets belong to reporting periods. A period is identified by its start and end dates, so quarters and years can sit side by side. Periods can be closed when collection ends and locked when the figures are final.

### Nothing inferred is shown as fact

ImpactMEL shows what was reported and approved. Where something is missing, it says so: a report prints "Explanation outstanding" or "No target set" rather than a guess. Text drafted by AI is marked as a draft and cannot be published until a person accepts it.

## Where ImpactMEL runs

ImpactMEL runs in our managed cloud, where each organisation has its own web address, for example `yourorganisation.impactmel.com`. It can also be installed on your own infrastructure. See [Security and data](/security) and [Plans](/plans).
