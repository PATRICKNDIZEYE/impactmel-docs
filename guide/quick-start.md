# Quick Start

From an empty workspace to your first tracked indicator in about fifteen minutes. This is the path every new organization walks — each step links to the full manual chapter and a walkthrough video.

## 1. Sign in and set up your organization

Sign in with the account from your welcome email. On first login, complete your organization profile — name, country, and default currency — since these flow into every report you'll produce. *([Getting Started →](/user-manual/getting-started))*

## 2. Create your first program

Programs are your funding streams. Go to **Programs → New Program**, name it, add the donor and timeline. *([Programs →](/user-manual/programs))*

## 3. Add a project under it

Projects are where delivery happens. **Programs → New Project**: pick the program, set dates, and assign a project manager. *([Projects →](/user-manual/projects))*

## 4. Build the results framework

Open the project's **Results Framework**, choose the structure your donor uses — Results Framework, Logframe, or Theory of Change — and build the chain from your impact statement down to outputs. *([Results Framework →](/user-manual/results-framework))*

## 5. Define an indicator with targets

From the project's **Indicators & Targets** tab, create your first indicator: name it with its unit, set the baseline, generate reporting periods, and add a target per period. *([Indicators →](/user-manual/indicators))*

## 6. Invite your team

**Settings → Members**: invite your M&E officers, reporters, and viewers with the right role each. Reporters can immediately start entering values. *([Roles →](/user-manual/roles-permissions))*

## 7. Run your first reporting cycle

A reporter enters the period's value and submits; a reviewer approves it from **Submissions**; the dashboard and indicator pages update on the spot. *([Entering Data →](/user-manual/data-entry))*
