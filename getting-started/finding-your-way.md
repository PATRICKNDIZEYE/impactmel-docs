# Finding your way

<p class="lead">Every person sees a short sidebar built for their role, a search bar that reaches any page they can open, and a user menu for their profile and settings.</p>

## The sidebar

The sidebar has up to three groups:

- **Work** holds the pages for your role, such as Home, Programmes, Review or Reports.
- **Programmes** or **My projects** lists the programmes or projects you work on, so you can open them in one click.
- **Organisation** lists the workspace pages your permissions open, such as People or Workspace.

Items you do not have permission for are hidden, not greyed out. Choose the panel icon at the top to collapse the sidebar to icons.

## Search

Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> on a Mac or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> on Windows, or choose **Search pages and programmes** at the top. Type the name of a page, programme or project and press Enter. Search only lists what you are allowed to open, including pages that are not in your sidebar, such as **Risks**.

## Home pages link to their detail

Every number and row on a home page opens the page behind it, already filtered. For example, "12 at risk" opens the Indicators page showing those 12 indicators.

## The user menu

Choose your name at the bottom of the sidebar.

![The user menu, with Work as for people who hold more than one role.](/images/user-menu.webp)

- **Your profile.** Your photo, name and job title, and the roles you hold.
- **Sign-in and security.** Your sign-in methods and password.
- **Help.** Opens this documentation.
- **Work as.** Appears only if you hold more than one role. It changes your home page and menu to that role. It does not change your permissions: you can always do everything your roles allow together.
- **Switch to classic interface.** Opens the previous ImpactMEL interface. Your choice is remembered, and you can switch back from the same menu or from your profile.
- **Sign out.**

## Light and dark

Choose the sun icon at the top right to switch between light and dark.

## On a phone

ImpactMEL works in a phone's browser. The sidebar becomes a menu button, and pages stack into one column. Field officers have a phone-first filling page for forms. See [Collecting data](/manual/collecting-data).
