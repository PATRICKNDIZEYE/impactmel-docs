# Invite your team

<p class="lead">People join a workspace by invitation. Each invitation carries the roles the person will have. Invitations are sent by email and last 7 days.</p>

## Invite people

You need permission to manage people. By default that is the Organisation admin.

1. Open **People** in the sidebar.
2. Choose **Invite people**.
3. Enter one or more email addresses, separated by commas. Everyone in one invitation gets the same roles.
4. Tick the roles they should have. The description under each role says what it can do.
5. Choose **Send invitation**.

![Inviting people and choosing their roles.](/images/people-invite.webp)

Only the owner can give someone the Organisation admin role. Other administrators can only give access they hold themselves.

## Manage invitations

The **Invitations** tab on the People page lists invitations that have not been accepted. For each one you can:

- **Send again**, if the email was missed.
- **Copy link**, to send it another way.
- **Cancel invitation**, if it was sent by mistake.

## Accept an invitation

When someone invites you, you receive an email from ImpactMEL.

1. Open the link in the email.
2. If you are new to ImpactMEL, enter your first name, last name and a password of at least 8 characters.
3. Choose **Accept and continue**.

If you already have an ImpactMEL account with the same email, sign in and choose **Accept and continue**. If you are signed in with a different email, sign out first.

An invitation that has expired, been withdrawn or already been used shows a message saying so. Ask the person who invited you to send it again.

## Which role to give

| Person | Role |
|---|---|
| Head of organisation, country director | Director |
| Head of MEL, M&E officer | M&E officer |
| Project or programme manager | Programme manager |
| Enumerator, data collector, field staff | Field officer |
| Finance manager, accountant | Finance officer |
| Board member, external reviewer, donor contact | Viewer |
| IT or office administrator | Organisation admin |

A person can hold more than one role. See [Roles at a glance](/roles/).
