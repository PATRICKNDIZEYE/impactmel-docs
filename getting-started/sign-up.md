# Sign up

<p class="lead">You can create an ImpactMEL workspace yourself in a few minutes. You choose a plan first, create your account, verify your email and then set up the workspace.</p>

::: tip Joining an existing workspace?
If a colleague has invited you, you do not need to sign up. Open the link in your invitation email instead. See [Accept an invitation](/getting-started/invite-your-team#accept-an-invitation).
:::

## Before you start

You need a work email address you can read, and a few minutes. Plans and prices are on the [pricing page](https://www.impactmel.com/pricing).

## Steps

Go to [app.impactmel.com/signup](https://app.impactmel.com/signup). A panel on the left shows the steps and where you are.

![The first step of sign-up: choose a plan, monthly or yearly.](/images/signup-plan.webp)

1. **Your plan.** Choose **Individual**, **Standard** or **Custom**, and **Monthly** or **Yearly**. If you came from the pricing page with a plan chosen, this step is skipped.
   - **Individual** is for one person reporting on their own work.
   - **Standard** is the full platform as it is, for an organisation that wants to start straight away.
   - **Custom** adds customisation of your framework, forms, reports and workflows, set up with us.
2. **Your details.** Enter your full name, work email and a password of at least 8 characters. You can also choose **Sign up with Google** or **Sign up with Microsoft**.
3. **Verify your email.** We send a 6-digit code to your email. Type it in. If it has not arrived, wait 30 seconds and choose **Resend code**.
4. **Your organisation.** Enter your organisation's name and choose its web address, for example `meridian.impactmel.com`. Individual plans skip this step: the workspace is named after you.
5. **Payment.** Choose how to pay:
   - **Pay by card** opens a secure checkout with our payment provider, Polar.
   - **Pay by invoice** asks where to send the invoice and an optional purchase order number. Invoices are not available on Individual.

When you finish, ImpactMEL opens your workspace at its own address and starts the set-up steps. Continue with [Set up your workspace](/getting-started/set-up-workspace).

## Signing in later

Sign in at [app.impactmel.com/login](https://app.impactmel.com/login) or at your organisation's own address.

![The sign-in page.](/images/login.webp)

1. Enter your work email and choose **Continue**.
2. Enter your password, or choose **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Microsoft**.
3. If you belong to more than one organisation, choose the one you want.

Microsoft work accounts are accepted when Microsoft confirms that the email domain belongs to your organisation. Otherwise ImpactMEL asks you to use your password.

### Forgotten password

Choose **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page and enter your email. The reset link works once, for one hour.

### Organisations on their own server

Some organisations run ImpactMEL on their own servers. If yours does, use the address your administrator gave you, or choose **Find its address** on the sign-in page.
