# Set up your workspace

<p class="lead">After sign-up, ImpactMEL takes the owner through four short steps: your role, your logo and currency, your reporting calendar, and your team. Each step can be skipped and changed later.</p>

The set-up opens with **Welcome**. Choose **Get started**, or **Skip for now** to go straight to your home page.

## Your role

Choose the role closest to your job: Director or senior manager, M&E officer, Programme or project manager, Field officer or data collector, Finance officer or accountant, System administrator, or Something else. Your home page follows this choice. As the person who created the workspace, you are its **owner** and keep full access whatever you choose here.

## Make the workspace yours

- **Upload logo.** PNG, JPG, WebP or SVG, up to 2 MB. The logo appears in the sidebar and on every report and public page.
- **Country.** ImpactMEL suggests one from your time zone.
- **Currency.** The reporting currency for budgets and spending.

## How often do you report?

Choose your **reporting frequency** (monthly, quarterly, every six months or yearly) and the month your **financial year starts in**. ImpactMEL shows a preview of your periods and creates them when you continue. You can add or change periods later. See [Reporting periods](/manual/reporting-periods).

## Invite your team

Add colleagues by email and choose the access each one gets: Admin, Director, M&E officer, Programme manager, Finance officer, Field officer or Viewer. Choose **Add another** for more rows, then **Send invites and finish**. Individual plans do not have this step.

## After set-up

Administrators see a **Getting started** card on their home page with the next things to do: invite your team, add your logo, set your reporting calendar and check what each role can do.

![The administrator's home page with the Getting started card.](/images/role-admin-home.webp)

In a new workspace with no programmes yet, M&E officers and programme managers see a **Get started** panel instead, with these steps in order:

1. Create your first programme. See [Programmes and projects](/manual/programmes-and-projects).
2. Build its results framework. See [Results framework](/manual/results-framework).
3. Add indicators and targets. See [Indicators, baselines and targets](/manual/indicators).
4. Invite your team. See [Invite your team](/getting-started/invite-your-team).
5. Collect your first figures. See [Collecting data](/manual/collecting-data).

All workspace settings can be changed later under **Workspace**. See [Administration](/manual/administration).
