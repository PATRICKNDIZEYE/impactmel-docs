# Release notes

<p class="lead">What changed in recent ImpactMEL releases, in plain language. Every organisation on the managed cloud runs the same release.</p>

## v0.10.36 · 10 November 2026

- Faster pages for large workspaces: long lists now load page by page.
- Notifications about figures to review go only to people who can review them.

## v0.10.35 · 10 November 2026

- **Progress is measured from the baseline on every screen**, the same way as in reports: (actual − baseline) ÷ (target − baseline). Without a baseline, progress is actual ÷ target. See [Indicators](/manual/indicators#the-progress-formula).

## v0.10.33 to v0.10.34 · 10 November 2026

- **New quarterly donor report format.** A one-page quarter at a glance, a results matrix with programme totals, what needs attention with owners and dates, delivery by project, a single risk register, next quarter, data notes and sign-off, and an annex. See [Reports](/manual/reports#the-quarterly-donor-report).
- **Report details panel** for the parts only your team can write: summary, requests to the donor, contact, story of change, actions and target dates.
- Demonstration workspaces mark every report "Sample data".
- Plans are now named **Individual**, **Standard** and **Custom**.

## v0.10.31 to v0.10.32 · 9 November 2026

- **Public dashboard links.** Share a dashboard with anyone, with an expiry date and a switch to turn it off. Small counts are hidden. See [Sharing dashboards](/manual/sharing-dashboards).
- Large organisation logos now show on public pages.

## v0.10.30 · 9 November 2026

- **Sign-up starts with the plan.** Individual plans skip the organisation name and web address.

## v0.10.27 to v0.10.29 · 7 to 8 November 2026

- **Funder workspace.** Portfolio, grantees, common indicators, requests with donor templates, review with accept and query, board pack export with a fingerprint, and a view log on both sides. See [Funder workspace](/manual/funders).
- Grantees see funder invitations, choose what to share, and answer requests from the Reports page.

## v0.10.25 to v0.10.26 · 6 to 7 November 2026

- Form responses: view, export to Excel or CSV, and delete.
- Participants: edit, delete, households, and import from CSV or Excel.
- Performance judgements per period in the results framework.
- Project places, and copy or delete programmes and projects with a preview of what is affected.
- Project registers: stakeholders and target groups, evaluations, period narratives and documents.
- Tighter access rules for roles limited to their own projects.

## v0.10.24 · 6 November 2026

- **Budgets and finance.** Budgets on the project page, funding agreements, versions and approval, spending, variance explanations, sign-off and programme roll-ups. New **Finance officer** role. See [Budgets and finance](/manual/budgets).
- **Risk register.** Heat map, reviews, escalation and decisions, donor risk scales. See [Risks](/manual/risks).
- **Period sign-off** in the new interface: close, lock and reopen with a reason.

## v0.10.21 to v0.10.23 · 4 November 2026

- Security releases: stricter permission and organisation checks across budgets, risks, organisations and related records.

## v0.10.13 to v0.10.20 · 3 to 4 November 2026

- **Analysis and dashboards.** Charts from approved figures, many chart types, maps, saved dashboards with drag and resize, and recommended dashboards.
- Reports: edit a draft before publishing, lock and fingerprint on publish, versions, and your logo on the report page.
- Every number on a home page opens the list behind it.
- Indicators without a target are no longer counted as off track.
- Participants export to CSV.

## v0.10.5 to v0.10.12 · 3 November 2026

- **AI assistant:** draft a form from a description, draft a results framework from a proposal, explain review checks, draft a report narrative. Administrators can switch it off.
- **Form logic:** show a question only when earlier answers call for it, checked on the server too.
- Programme and project pages.
- Automatic data-quality checks in review.

## v0.10.0 to v0.10.4 · 1 to 2 November 2026

- **The new interface (version 2)** is the default, with a home page and menu for each role, and a switch back to the classic interface.
- Self-service sign-up with card or invoice payment, and a web address for each organisation.
- Sign in with Google or Microsoft.
- Branded emails.
