# Results framework

<p class="lead">The results framework sets out what each programme intends to achieve, as a chain from impact to outcomes to outputs. Indicators are linked to the results they measure, so progress rolls up the chain.</p>

**Who can do this:** M&E officers and Organisation admins build the framework. Directors and M&E officers record performance judgements.

## Open the framework

Open **Results framework** in the sidebar. Choose the programme, and optionally a project and a reporting period. The summary shows how many results there are at each level, how many indicators are linked, and how results stand against the chosen period's targets.

![The results framework: impact, outcomes and outputs, with linked indicators and status.](/images/framework.webp)

The framework is a chain of **Impact**, **Outcome** and **Output** results. It is used for results frameworks and logframes alike: each result can carry its assumptions, risks and target group.

## Add a result

1. Choose **Add a result**. In an empty framework, choose **Build the framework**.
2. Choose the project and level, and which result it **contributes to**. Outputs sit under an outcome.
3. Enter the title, code and result statement, and optionally assumptions, risks and target group.
4. Save.

## Link indicators

Open a result and use **Indicators that measure this result**, then **Save links**. Each indicator measures one result. Linking an indicator to another result moves it, with its targets and figures.

## Performance judgements

Figures show how far an indicator moved. A judgement records whether the result was achieved, and why. Open a result and choose **Judge**.

- Choose the period, or **Whole plan, at the end**.
- Choose a rating: **Over-achieved**, **Achieved**, **Partially achieved**, **Not achieved** or **Too early to judge**.
- Write the reason. ImpactMEL shows **What the figures say** beside it. You can add evidence and **What happens next**.
- **Save draft** or **Record as final**. A final judgement can be reopened as a draft with **Reopen as draft**.

Judgements appear in reports under "Review of results".

## Draft a framework from a proposal

If AI features are on, M&E officers can choose **Draft from a proposal**.

![Drafting a framework from a proposal document.](/images/framework-ai.webp)

1. Choose the project and upload a PDF or Word document up to 15 MB. Scanned PDFs do not work, because they hold no text.
2. Choose **Read the document**.
3. ImpactMEL proposes results and indicators, each with the sentence it came from. For each one choose **Yes, add it** or **Leave it out**.
4. Choose **Add results and indicators**.

Anything the document did not make clear is saved on the indicator under **To confirm with your team**, as a question rather than a fact. The document itself is not stored. See [AI features](/manual/ai).
