# Budgets and finance

<p class="lead">ImpactMEL keeps each project's budget, funding and spending beside its results. It is a control layer over your accounting system: spending comes from your accounts, typed in or imported, and ImpactMEL tracks it against plan, asks for explanations and records sign-off.</p>

**Who can do this:** Finance officers and Programme managers keep budgets and record spending. Directors approve budget versions and sign off periods. M&E officers can read budgets.

## Where budgets are

The **Budget** section sits on each project page. Programme pages roll their projects up, and the Director's Overview shows overall spending.

![A project budget: approved budget, spent to date, time gone and forecast, with budget against actual by line.](/images/project-budget.webp)

The section has four tabs: **Budget vs actual**, **Spending**, **Versions** and **Funding**.

## Set up a budget

1. On the project page, choose **Set up a budget**.
2. Choose the currency, and phase the budget by month, quarter, half-year or year. Both are fixed once a version is approved.
3. Add headings and lines. Each line can have a funding source and a phasing; **Spread evenly** divides it across periods. An indirect costs line can be calculated from the funding agreement's rate.

## Funding

Under **Funding**, choose **Add funding** and enter the funder or source, agreement number, type (grant, cost share or in-kind), the committed amount, and whether it is the main agreement for this budget. Each agreement also sets:

- **Flexibility rule**, how far money can move between lines or headings, as a percentage;
- **Explain variance beyond (±%)**, the threshold that triggers an explanation, usually 10%;
- **Indirect cost rate (%)**.

## Versions and approval

A draft budget is sent with **Submit for approval**. A Director chooses **Approve version** or **Return for changes**. Approved versions are read-only. **Revise budget** starts a new version and asks for a reason. A forecast is kept as its own version.

## Record spending

- **Record spending.** Choose the line and period and enter the amount. If it was paid in another currency, enter the rate and its source. The rate is kept as entered.
- **Import from Excel.** Upload CSV or Excel ledger rows, up to 5 MB.
- **Correct a locked period** adds a dated adjustment rather than changing the original.

## Variance explanations

Any line whose spending differs from plan by more than the agreement's threshold needs an explanation. Choose **Write explanation**. Lines without one appear in the Finance officer's **Explanations due** and in reports as "Explanation outstanding".

## Sign off a period

When a period's spending is complete, choose **Sign off** for that period. This locks its figures, and reports state that spending was signed off.

## Programme roll-up

The programme page shows each project's budget, spending, spending against time gone, forecast and lines to explain.
