# Funder workspace

<p class="lead">Funders who support several organisations can have their own ImpactMEL workspace. They ask grantees for results, and grantees choose what to share. Funders see only what has been shared, in a locked copy.</p>

Funder workspaces are set up by ImpactMEL on request. Contact [inquiries@impactmel.com](mailto:inquiries@impactmel.com).

## Funder roles

Funder admin, M&E lead, Programme officer and Board viewer. The menu has four pages: **Portfolio**, **Grantees**, **Requests** and **Reports**.

## Portfolio

![The funder portfolio.](/images/funder-portfolio.webp)

Choose a request to see where every grantee stands: requested, in progress, submitted, accepted, queried and late. **Results** adds figures up only where the indicator units match; the rest are listed side by side. The portfolio also lists flagged risks and what grantees said in their own words. **Export PDF** and **Export Excel** produce a board pack with a fingerprint.

## Grantees

![Grantees and the indicators each one has mapped.](/images/funder-grantees.webp)

**Invite a grantee** with the organisation, email, grant reference, grant dates and a message. The invitation lasts 14 days. Each grantee page shows how their indicators map to yours, what they have submitted, and what each side has viewed. **End link** stops the sharing.

## Requests and common indicators

![Requests sent to grantees.](/images/funder-requests.webp)

- **Common indicators** are the indicators the funder wants every grantee to answer.
- **New request** asks grantees for one period: indicators and questions, or a light check-in. Start from a donor template, add up to eight questions, optionally ask for risks and a financial summary, choose the grantees and a due date.
- On a request, **Remind** grantees who have not shared. On a submission, **Accept** it or send a question.

## The grantee side

When a funder invites your organisation, an administrator reads the data-sharing agreement and accepts or declines.

- In **Workspace**, **Funders you share with** shows each funder, the programmes in scope, and how your indicators answer theirs: "One of ours answers it" or "It doesn't apply to our work". Small counts are hidden. You can see what the funder viewed, and end the link.
- Requests arrive under **Reports**, in the **Funder requests** tab. Each request shows what is asked, your answers (saved as you type), a preview, questions and replies, and the versions you have shared.
- You share from the sign-off of a locked period, in four steps: what is included, answers, a time check and confirm. The funder receives a locked copy with a fingerprint.
