# AI features

<p class="lead">ImpactMEL's AI assistant helps with drafting. It works from your own data, marks what it writes as a draft, and never changes a figure. A person always checks and accepts its work, and an administrator can switch it off for the whole workspace.</p>

## What it does

| Feature | Where | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Draft a form | Forms, **New form**, **Describe it**; **Ask AI** in the builder | Questions and skip logic from a plain description. Nothing is created until you accept |
| Draft a framework | Results framework, **Draft from a proposal** | Results and indicators from a proposal or concept note, each with its source sentence. You accept each one |
| Explain checks | Review, **Explain with AI** | A short explanation of the data-quality checks on a figure, with questions to ask the sender |
| Report narrative | Reports, **Draft from approved figures** | A narrative from the report's approved figures |
| Recommended dashboards | Analysis, **Recommended for you** | Ready-made dashboards from your approved figures |

## The rules it follows

- **Your data only.** It works from what is in your workspace or the document you upload. Uploaded documents are not stored.
- **Drafts, not facts.** AI text is labelled as a draft. A report cannot be published while any section is still an AI draft.
- **No invented numbers.** Explanations and narratives may quote only figures that exist in the submission or report. Sentences with other numbers are removed.
- **Questions, not guesses.** Where a proposal is unclear, the question is saved for your team to confirm.
- **Figures are never changed.** AI cannot enter, edit or approve figures.

There is a daily limit on AI use for each organisation, which resets at midnight UTC.

## Switch AI off

An Organisation admin can switch every AI feature off for everyone.

1. Open **Workspace**.
2. Under **AI assistant**, turn the switch off.

![The AI assistant switch in Workspace settings.](/images/workspace-ai.webp)

When AI is off, the AI buttons disappear for everyone in the workspace, and the server refuses AI requests. The change is recorded in the activity log. Turn it on again the same way.
