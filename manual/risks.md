# Risks

<p class="lead">The risk register records what could stop the work, how serious it is now, who looks after it and what is being done. Risks link to results and indicators, appear in deadlines and on home pages, and can be included in reports.</p>

**Who can do this:** Programme managers, M&E officers and Directors add and review risks. Directors decide on escalated risks. Organisation admins configure risk settings.

## The Risks page

Open **Risks** from a home page tile, a project or programme page, or search.

![The Risks page: heat map and register.](/images/risks.webp)

- **Heat map.** Active risks by likelihood and impact. Choose a square to list its risks.
- **The risk register.** Filter by Active, Critical, High, Reviews overdue, Actions overdue, Waiting for a decision, Occurred, Closed or All, and by project and category.
- **Export** as an EU risk table or a risk matrix with logframe reference.

## Add a risk

Choose **Add risk** and enter:

- where it applies (projects or a whole programme) and the risk in one line;
- cause, event and effect: **Because of**, **This could happen**, **Leading to**;
- category, likelihood from 1 (Rare) to 5 (Almost certain) and impact from 1 (Negligible) to 5 (Severe);
- owner, next review date and status;
- the result, indicator or assumption it threatens;
- the response (avoid, reduce, transfer, share, accept or fallback plan), what is already being done, and actions with a person and date;
- what happens if it occurs anyway, and a target rating;
- **Share with the funder**, off by default.

The score is likelihood times impact: Low 1 to 4, Medium 5 to 9, High 10 to 15, Critical 16 to 25. High and critical risks are reviewed monthly by default, others every three months.

## Review a risk

![A risk's detail panel.](/images/risk-detail.webp)

Open a risk and choose **Review** to record its current rating. The panel keeps a score history. Other actions: **Edit**, **It happened**, **Close** and **Remove**.

## Escalate and decide

**Escalate** sends a risk to the Director. The Director chooses **Decide**: accept it, treat it further, or return it. Risks waiting for a decision show on the Director's Overview.

## Risk settings

Organisation admins set the level names (Standard, EU, World Bank and UNDP, or Global Fund), categories and appetite in **Workspace**, under **Risks**. **Start from a donor's list** fills in a donor's categories.
