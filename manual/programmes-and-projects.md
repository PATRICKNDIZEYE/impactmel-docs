# Programmes and projects

<p class="lead">A programme groups related projects, usually under one strategy or donor. Projects are where indicators, activities, budgets and teams live. Programme pages roll their projects up.</p>

**Who can do this:** anyone with programmes or projects permission can read; Organisation admins, M&E officers and Programme managers can create and edit by default.

## The Programmes page

Open **Programmes** in the sidebar. Each programme shows its projects, a timeline, budget and spending, framework status, indicators on track, locations and overall status.

![The Programmes page: every programme and its projects on one screen.](/images/programmes-list.webp)

- Filter by donor, sector or status (**On track**, **At risk**, **Off track**).
- Switch between **List** and **Map** to see where projects work.
- **Expand all** or **Collapse all** shows or hides each programme's projects.

## Create a programme

1. On the Programmes page, choose **New programme**.
2. Enter the name, code, status (**In the pipeline**, **Active** or **Closed**), donor, sector, start and end dates, and a description.
3. Save.

## Create a project

1. Choose **New project** on the Programmes page or on a programme page.
2. Choose the programme, then enter the name, code, start and end dates, approved budget for the project's whole life and its currency.
3. Choose the **Project manager** and the team, and add locations.
4. Save.

## The programme page

![A programme page, with its budget roll-up and projects.](/images/programme-page.webp)

A programme page shows totals for projects, timeline, budget and indicators on track, then the programme budget roll-up, the projects table, a description, risks and what is due soon. Use **Edit programme** to change its details.

## The project page

![A project page: indicators, budget, activities, risks and registers in one place.](/images/project-page.webp)

The project page has two columns.

- **Main column:** Indicators, Budget, Activities, Risks, and the registers: Stakeholders and target groups, Period narratives, Evaluations and Documents. See [Participants and registers](/manual/participants#project-registers).
- **Side column:** About this project, Team, Locations, Reports and shortcuts.

Project status can be Planning, Active, On hold, Completed or Cancelled.

### Places

In **Edit project**, under locations, choose **Add a place**. A place can be new, one already used by another project, or an administrative area you search for. You can add latitude and longitude or click the map, choose the coverage (one site, district, region, whole country and so on), and mark one place as the project's main location.

## Copy a programme or project

From the **…** menu, choose **Start a programme from this one** or **Start a project from this one**. The copy takes the results framework and indicators. Targets, periods, figures, budgets and judgements are not copied. For a programme you can also copy its projects, risks and evaluations; for a project, its places, team, target groups, stakeholders and open risks.

## Delete a programme or project

From the **…** menu, choose **Delete**. ImpactMEL first lists everything that would be deleted with it. Larger deletions ask you to type the name to confirm. Deletion cannot be undone, and ImpactMEL refuses it where approved figures, final judgements or published reports would be lost.
