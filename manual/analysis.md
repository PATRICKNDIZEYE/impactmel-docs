# Analysis and dashboards

<p class="lead">Analysis turns approved figures into charts. Pick indicators and how to break them down, and ImpactMEL draws the clearest chart for them. Charts can be saved to dashboards that re-run with the latest figures each time they open.</p>

**Who can do this:** anyone with indicator permission can analyse. M&E officers and Organisation admins save and share dashboards by default.

## Build a chart

Open **Analysis** in the sidebar.

![A chart built in Analysis.](/images/analysis-chart.webp)

1. Under **Indicators**, choose up to eight indicators.
2. Under **Break down by**, choose nothing (one total), period, project, location or a disaggregation such as sex. A single indicator can be broken down twice.
3. Under **Show**, choose **Actual**, **vs target** or **% of target**.
4. Filter by programme or project and period: this year, last four quarters, last 12 months, all time or a custom range.

ImpactMEL picks a chart. You can switch to another that fits: figures, line, area, bars, above or below target, progress to target, donut, treemap, grouped, stacked or 100% stacked bars, stacked area, heatmap, scatter, map, small multiples or a table.

Approved figures only are used, unless you switch on **Include figures awaiting approval**; those are drawn paler. Download a chart as an image (PNG) or its data as CSV.

## Recommended dashboards

If AI features are on, **Recommended for you** offers ready-made dashboards built from your approved figures. Every chart is checked against those figures before it is shown. Choose **Preview** to look, and **Save dashboard** to keep one.

![A recommended dashboard before saving.](/images/analysis-recommended.webp)

## Dashboards

Choose **Save to dashboard** on any chart, or open **Dashboards** from the Analysis page.

![A saved dashboard.](/images/dashboard.webp)

- **Edit layout** lets you drag and resize charts on a computer.
- **Add a chart**, **Rename dashboard** and **Delete dashboard**.
- Dashboard filters re-scope every chart at once; **as saved** keeps each chart's own settings.

To share a dashboard outside your workspace, see [Sharing dashboards](/manual/sharing-dashboards).

## Locations

Directors and Viewers have a **Locations** page: a map of where forms were received, participants registered and activities held, by place. District views need boundary data for each country; where it is not loaded, places show as points.
