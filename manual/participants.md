# Participants and registers

<p class="lead">The participant registry holds the people your activities reach and what each person attended. It is the source behind reach figures broken down by sex, age and disability. Projects also keep registers of stakeholders, evaluations, narratives and documents.</p>

**Who can do this:** M&E officers and Organisation admins by default. Anyone with participants permission can read the registry.

## The Participants page

Open **Participants** in the sidebar.

![The Participants page, with reach broken down by sex, age and disability.](/images/participants.webp)

The tiles show people reached, the share who are female, youth aged 15 to 24, and people with a disability. Filter by sex, age group, disability and activity, or search by name, household or ID.

## Register someone

Choose **Register someone**. Only the name is required. You can also record sex, date of birth, age group, disability and its type, phone, national ID, email, village or site, household code, and whether they head the household.

## A participant's record

Open a person to see their details, the activities they are registered for and whether they attended, and their household. From here you can:

- **Register for an activity** and record attendance as Attended, Absent or Registered;
- add or remove household members;
- **Edit details**, or **Delete record**. A person with attendance cannot be deleted.

## Import participants

Choose **Import** and upload a CSV or Excel file. Download the **Excel template** or **CSV template** first. Before anything is saved, the preview counts new people, duplicates and problems. A duplicate is the same national ID, or the same name, date of birth and village. Link household members with the **Household member of** column.

## Export

**Export CSV** downloads the list as filtered.

## Project registers

Each project page has four registers.

- **Stakeholders and target groups.** Stakeholders with their type, role, agreement and contact, placed on an influence and interest grid; target groups with an estimated size.
- **Evaluations.** Plan an evaluation (baseline to learning review) with its status, dates, evaluator and budget, then record findings, lessons learned, recommendations and the management response.
- **Period narratives.** One narrative per indicator per period, explaining what happened. Reports use them.
- **Documents.** Upload a file up to 50 MB or link one, typed as report, plan, evidence, photo or other, with tags.
