# Sharing dashboards

<p class="lead">A dashboard can be shared with anyone through a public, read-only link. The link shows approved figures only, expires when you choose, and can be turned off at any time.</p>

**Who can do this:** people who can save dashboards, by default M&E officers and Organisation admins. Roles limited to assigned projects cannot create public links.

## Create a link

1. Open the dashboard and choose **Share**.
2. Under **Link stops working after**, choose 30 days, 90 days, 1 year or Never. The default is 90 days.
3. Choose **Create link**. The link is copied, ready to send.

![Sharing a dashboard: choose when the link expires.](/images/dashboard-share.webp)

The Share panel lists each active link with who made it, when, when it expires and how many times it has been viewed.

![An active dashboard link.](/images/dashboard-share-link.webp)

## Turn a link off

Choose **Turn off** beside the link and confirm. It stops working for everyone at once.

## What the public sees

![A shared dashboard as the public sees it.](/images/public-dashboard.webp)

- Your organisation's name and logo, the dashboard title and its charts.
- The period, the date the figures are as of, and "Data approved by" your organisation.

## What the public does not see

- Figures awaiting approval.
- Internal notes and identifiers.
- Breakdown counts under 5, which are hidden to protect individuals. Charts that would reveal them switch to bars.

Figures on a shared dashboard refresh within about a minute of an approval.
