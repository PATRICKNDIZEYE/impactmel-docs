# Administration

<p class="lead">Administration covers who is in the workspace, what each person can do, how the workspace is set up, what has changed, and how it is paid for.</p>

**Who can do this:** Organisation admins. Other roles see the workspace pages their permissions open, in the **Organisation** group of the sidebar.

## People

Open **People**.

![The People page.](/images/people.webp)

The **Members** tab lists everyone with their roles and when they last signed in. **Invite people** sends invitations; see [Invite your team](/getting-started/invite-your-team). From a member's **…** menu:

- **Change roles.** Tick the roles they hold and choose which one their home **opens on**.
- **Make owner.** Transfers ownership. Type "transfer" to confirm. Only the owner can do this.
- **Remove from workspace.** Removes their access. Their past work and history stay.

## Roles and permissions

Open **Roles and permissions**.

![Roles and permissions.](/images/roles-permissions.webp)

- **One role** shows a role's permissions, grouped by area. **Compare all** shows every role side by side.
- Tick or untick permissions to change a role. Built-in roles can be changed, and **Reset to defaults** restores them.
- **New role** creates a role, starting from an existing one. **Rename** and **Delete role** manage your own roles.

Permission groups: Programmes and projects, Results and indicators, Data collection, Review and sign-off, Reports and dashboards, Budgets and spending, Risks, Funders, Integrations, People and access, Workspace, and Records.

Only owners can grant Organisation admin. Anyone else can only give access they already have, and cannot edit a role they hold.

## Workspace settings

Open **Workspace**.

![Workspace settings.](/images/workspace-settings.webp)

- **Identity:** logo, organisation name, country and reporting currency. The logo shows in the sidebar, on every exported report and on public pages.
- **Reporting calendar:** when your financial year starts and how often you report. See [Reporting periods](/manual/reporting-periods).
- **Funders you share with.** See [Funder workspace](/manual/funders#the-grantee-side).
- **AI assistant:** on or off for everyone. See [AI features](/manual/ai#switch-ai-off).
- **Risks:** level names, categories and appetite. See [Risks](/manual/risks#risk-settings).

## Activity log

Open **Activity log** to see who did what, and when, newest first.

![The activity log.](/images/activity-log.webp)

Filter by person, what they did, what it was done to, and dates. The log records changes to people and roles, figures sent, approved and returned, periods closed and locked, reports created and published, and more. Each change keeps the value before it.

## Sign-in and security

Open **Sign-in and security** from the sidebar or the user menu.

![Sign-in and security.](/images/security-settings.webp)

It shows which sign-in methods your workspace offers (email and password, Google, Microsoft), and lets you change your password.

## Plans and billing

Open **Plans and billing**.

![Plans and billing.](/images/billing.webp)

- **Current plan** shows your plan, and its status (Invoice requested, Active, Payment overdue, Cancelled, or Paid by your funder).
- **Change plan** or **Choose a plan** to move between Individual, Standard and Custom.
- **Pay by card** opens checkout with Polar, which emails receipts. **Request an invoice** asks for an invoice instead.

Prices are on the [pricing page](https://www.impactmel.com/pricing). See [Plans](/plans).

## Your profile

Open **Your profile** from the user menu to change your photo, name and job title, see your roles, and choose between the new and classic interface.
