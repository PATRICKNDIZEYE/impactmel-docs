# Reporting periods

<p class="lead">Reporting periods are the calendar that figures and targets belong to. They are shared by every project in the workspace and identified by their start and end dates, so quarters, years and one-off periods can sit side by side.</p>

**Who can do this:** Organisation admins set the reporting calendar. Organisation admins, Directors and M&E officers add, close, lock and reopen periods.

## The reporting calendar

In **Workspace**, under **Reporting calendar**, choose the month your **financial year starts in** and how often **you report**: monthly, quarterly, every six months or yearly. Saving adds this year's missing periods and keeps the ones you already have.

## Manage periods

On the M&E officer's home page, the **Reporting periods** panel shows each open period, with how many indicators have a figure in, how many are approved and what is outstanding. Choose **Manage** to see every period.

![The Reporting periods dialog.](/images/periods-dialog.webp)

### Add periods

Choose **Add reporting periods**, then:

- **Quarters**, **Months** or **Years**, with a **From** and **To** range; or
- **One period**, with a name, first day and last day.

The same dates always mean the same period, so two projects reporting on 1 April to 30 June share one period.

## Close, lock and reopen

A period moves through three states:

| State | What it means |
|---|---|
| Open | Figures can be entered, sent and corrected |
| Closed | No new figures can be entered. Figures already sent can still be reviewed |
| Locked | The figures are final |

Before you close a period, ImpactMEL lists what is outstanding:

- figures still waiting for a decision;
- figures sent back and not yet resubmitted;
- indicators due with nothing reported;
- results not yet judged for the period.

When nothing is outstanding it says **Nothing outstanding; ready to close**.

- **Close period** stops new figures.
- **Lock** makes the figures final. Only a closed period can be locked.
- **Reopen** asks for a written reason. If a funder holds a shared copy of that period, ImpactMEL tells you a correction may follow.

A period can be renamed, have its dates changed or be deleted only while it is open. A period that holds figures cannot be deleted. The server refuses figures for a closed or locked period.
