# Collecting data

<p class="lead">Figures reach ImpactMEL in four ways: forms filled on a phone or computer, figures entered directly against an indicator, spreadsheet imports, and integrations. All of them arrive for review before they count.</p>

## Fill a form on a phone

Field officers open **Forms** in the sidebar to see every published form, and choose **Fill**. The form opens on its own page, designed for a phone. The same page opens from a form's public link, for partners without an account.

![A form open on a phone.](/images/phone-form-pair.webp)

- The organisation's logo and the form name are at the top, with a counter of answered questions.
- Questions marked * need an answer.
- Location questions offer **Use my location**. Evidence questions offer **Take a photo or choose a file**.
- Choose **Send response**. The page confirms with **Response sent**.

If you are signed in when you send, ImpactMEL records who sent it. Responses from the public link are recorded as sent through the shared link.

### What happens without a connection

ImpactMEL forms work in the phone's browser. There is no app to install. Be clear with field teams about what this means:

- **Opening a form needs a connection.** The page cannot be opened, or installed, offline.
- **Once open, answers are kept on the phone.** They are saved in the browser as you type. If the page is closed and reopened on the same phone and browser, ImpactMEL offers the answers you started.
- **Sending waits for a connection.** If there is no connection when you choose **Send response**, the page says **Waiting for a connection… Keep this page open.** It tries again every 20 seconds and as soon as the phone reconnects. A response is never sent twice.
- **Limits.** One unsent response per form per phone. A photo chosen while offline is held only until the page is closed. Clearing the browser's data removes saved answers.

For long trips without coverage, use a tool built for offline collection, such as KoboToolbox, and bring the submissions in through the [KoboToolbox integration](/manual/integrations#kobotoolbox).

## Enter a figure

Field officers can enter a figure directly against an indicator.

1. On **My tasks**, choose **New figure**.
2. Choose the project, the indicator and the period. Only open periods without a figure from you are offered.
3. Choose **Start**, enter the values and any breakdown, mark estimates, and add evidence.
4. Choose **Send for review**, or **Save without sending** to finish later.

![Starting a new figure.](/images/field-new-figure.webp)

## Correct a returned figure

When a reviewer returns a figure, it appears first on **My tasks** with their reason.

1. Choose **Correct**.
2. The page shows the reason, the values, and what each value was before. Change the values, tick **Estimated** where needed, and add a note.
3. Add evidence under **Evidence** with **Add a file**: photos, PDF, Word, Excel or video, up to 50 MB.
4. Choose **Send again**.

Only the person who entered a figure can correct it. A closed period blocks changes, and approved figures are final.

## See what you sent

**Sent** lists every figure you have sent, with tabs **All**, **Waiting**, **Returned** and **Approved**.

![The Sent page.](/images/field-sent.webp)

## Other ways in

- **Spreadsheets.** Import figures for many indicators at once from Excel or CSV. See [Integrations](/manual/integrations#excel-and-csv).
- **KoboToolbox and DHIS2.** See [Integrations](/manual/integrations).

Whatever the route, figures only count once approved in [Review](/manual/review).
