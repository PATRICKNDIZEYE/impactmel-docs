# Review and data quality

<p class="lead">Every figure passes through one review queue before it counts. Reviewers see the figure beside its history, evidence and automatic checks, then approve it or return it with a reason. Approved figures are locked and feed dashboards and reports.</p>

**Who can do this:** Organisation admins, Directors and M&E officers approve and return figures by default.

## The review queue

Open **Review** in the sidebar. The number beside it is how many figures are waiting for you.

![The review queue: figures waiting for a decision, oldest first.](/images/review-queue.webp)

| Tab | Shows |
|---|---|
| All | Everything waiting for a decision |
| Asked to look | Figures a colleague asked you to look at |
| Waiting 5+ days | Figures waiting five days or more |
| Returned | Figures sent back, with the reason |
| Not sent | Drafts not yet sent, and who they are waiting for |
| History | Past decisions, from the activity log |

Each row shows the indicator, project and period, the figure, who sent it, when, and how many approvals it has, such as "1 of 2".

## Review a figure

Open a figure from the queue.

![A figure under review: values, evidence, approvals and checks.](/images/review-detail.webp)

- **Submitted values**, with breakdowns, estimates and notes.
- **Period narrative**, what the team says happened.
- **Evidence**, photos and files attached by the sender. Photos open full size with the time they were taken.
- **Comments.** Reply, or type @ to mention someone.
- **Approval**, who has approved and who is still to decide.
- **Checks**, the automatic data-quality checks.
- **Audit trail**, every step in order, numbered by round.

Use the arrows at the top to move to the previous or next figure.

## Automatic checks

ImpactMEL checks each figure against its definition and its history. Checks marked **Look at this** need attention; **Worth knowing** checks are for information.

- Percentage outside 0 to 100, or a value below zero
- Estimated without saying how
- Numerator larger than denominator, or a denominator of zero
- Breakdown that does not add up to the total
- Sent after the due date
- A running total that went down
- Much higher or lower than recent periods
- Out of line with a steady series
- The same figure as before
- Far above or far below the target

Comparisons use earlier approved figures only.

### Explain with AI

If AI features are on and a check needs attention, **Explain with AI** writes a short explanation of what the checks found and questions you could ask the sender. It is marked **AI-drafted** and quotes only numbers from the submission. **Use as comment** puts the questions in the thread.

![An AI explanation of the checks, with questions for the sender.](/images/review-ai.webp)

## Approve

Choose **Approve**, add an optional note and confirm. Each approver decides once per round.

## Return

Choose **Return** and say what needs to change. The sender is told by email and in ImpactMEL, and the figure appears first on their task list.

![Returning a figure with a reason.](/images/review-return.webp)

## Ask a colleague

Choose **Ask a colleague to look** and pick someone who knows the site better. They are told straight away, and the figure appears under **Asked to look** for them.

![Asking a colleague to look at a figure.](/images/review-ask.webp)

## Two approvers

A project can require two approvals before a figure is final. The queue then shows "1 of 2", and the figure is published after the second approval. Two approvals are set per project. There is no screen for this setting yet; ask ImpactMEL support to switch it on for a project.

## After approval

An approved figure is locked. It counts in dashboards, progress and reports for its period, and cannot be edited or deleted, so check it before you approve. Every decision, comment and change stays in the figure's history and in the [activity log](/manual/administration#activity-log).

## Close the period

When a period's figures are reviewed, close and lock it. See [Reporting periods](/manual/reporting-periods#close-lock-and-reopen).
