# Activities and deadlines

<p class="lead">Activities are the work plan: trainings, distributions, meetings and construction, with dates, a responsible person and participant targets. The Deadlines page brings every date that matters into one list.</p>

**Who can do this:** Programme managers and M&E officers create and update activities. Anyone with project permission can read them.

## The Activities page

Open **Activities** in the sidebar.

![The Activities page.](/images/activities.webp)

Use the tabs **To do**, **Overdue**, **Done** and **All**, the project filter and **Assigned to me**. Overdue means the planned end date has passed and the work is not marked done. Choose **Update** on a row to change its status or progress. Programme managers can also tick activities done from their home page.

## Add an activity

Choose **New activity** and enter:

- project, name, code and type (training, meeting, distribution, assessment, campaign, construction or other);
- planned start and end, status (planned, in progress, completed or cancelled) and percent complete;
- actual start and finish;
- description and venue;
- participant targets: total, women, men and youth;
- remarks, and who it is **assigned to**.

## The activity page

![An activity page.](/images/activity-detail.webp)

The activity page shows its dates, details, implementing partner, sub-activities, the result it delivers, and participants registered against target with the number who attended. Attendance is recorded on each participant's record. See [Participants](/manual/participants#a-participant-s-record).

## Deadlines

Open **Deadlines** in the sidebar.

![The Deadlines page.](/images/deadlines.webp)

Deadlines lists reporting periods with figures outstanding, activity end dates, risk actions and reviews, and calendar dates, grouped as **Overdue**, **Next 30 days** and **Later**. Filter by project.
