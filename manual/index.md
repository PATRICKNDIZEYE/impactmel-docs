# User manual

<p class="lead">The manual is organised by task, in the order the work happens: plan, collect, check, report. Each page says who can do the task, where to find it and what to expect.</p>

## Plan

- [Programmes and projects](/manual/programmes-and-projects): create, edit, copy and delete programmes and projects; project places.
- [Results framework](/manual/results-framework): impact, outcomes and outputs; linking indicators; performance judgements; drafting from a proposal.
- [Indicators, baselines and targets](/manual/indicators): defining an indicator, targets by period, how progress and status are worked out.
- [Reporting periods](/manual/reporting-periods): the reporting calendar, adding periods, closing and locking.

## Collect

- [Forms and the form builder](/manual/forms): field types, logic, location, photos, the AI assistant, publishing, responses.
- [Collecting data](/manual/collecting-data): the phone filling page and collection links, offline behaviour, entering and correcting figures.
- [Participants and registers](/manual/participants): the participant registry, households, imports, and project registers.
- [Activities and deadlines](/manual/activities): planning and updating activities, and the deadlines list.

## Check

- [Review and data quality](/manual/review): the review queue, automatic checks, approve and return, two approvers, evidence and history.
- [Budgets and finance](/manual/budgets): budgets, funding, spending, variance explanations and sign-off.
- [Risks](/manual/risks): the risk register, heat map, reviews, escalation and decisions.

## Report and share

- [Analysis and dashboards](/manual/analysis): charts from approved figures, saved dashboards, recommended dashboards.
- [Sharing dashboards](/manual/sharing-dashboards): public links, expiry, revoking, and what the public sees.
- [Reports](/manual/reports): report types, the quarterly donor format, Report details, publishing with a fingerprint, exports and share links.
- [Funder workspace](/manual/funders): portfolio, grantees, requests and what grantees share.

## Connect and administer

- [Integrations](/manual/integrations): KoboToolbox, DHIS2, and Excel or CSV import.
- [AI features](/manual/ai): what the AI assistant does, its limits, and how to switch it off.
- [Administration](/manual/administration): people, roles and permissions, workspace settings, activity log, sign-in and security, plans and billing.
