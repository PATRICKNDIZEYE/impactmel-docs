# Plans

<p class="lead">ImpactMEL is priced by where it runs and whether you want it customised. The <a href="https://www.impactmel.com/pricing">pricing page</a> is the source of truth for plans and prices; this page explains how plans work in the product.</p>

## Cloud plans

| Plan | For | Customisation |
|---|---|---|
| **Individual** | M&E consultants and officers who report on their own work | None |
| **Standard** | An organisation that wants the full platform as it is and to start straight away | None, the product as it is |
| **Custom** | An organisation that wants ImpactMEL set up around how it already works | Your framework, forms, reports and workflows, configured and set up with us |

Every cloud plan includes results frameworks, forms, approvals, evidence, dashboards, and PDF, Word and Excel reports. Standard and Custom run the same platform. There is no charge per project, indicator, record, participant or file.

Cloud plans can be paid monthly or yearly, by card or, except on Individual, by invoice.

## On your own servers

Organisation-hosted plans run ImpactMEL on your infrastructure, with training, documentation and support. They start with a conversation. See the [pricing page](https://www.impactmel.com/pricing) or write to [inquiries@impactmel.com](mailto:inquiries@impactmel.com).

## In the product

- Choose your plan when you [sign up](/getting-started/sign-up).
- Change plan or payment method in [Plans and billing](/manual/administration#plans-and-billing).
- An Individual workspace is one person holding every role, with nothing missing.
